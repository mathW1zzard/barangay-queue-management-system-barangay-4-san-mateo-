1. PURPOSE OF THE SYSTEM

This software manages client queueing, service records and attendance for the barangay office. It stores personal information of clients and staff on the computer where it is installed.

3. DATA PRIVACY (RA 10173)

By using this system, the barangay and its authorized staff agree to:

  a. Collect and record only the personal information needed to serve the client (transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality).
  b. Keep all client records confidential and use them only for official barangay purposes.
  c. Not disclose, copy, export or print client records for any purpose other than official barangay business, unless allowed by law or with the client's consent.
  d. Keep the administrator password and the recovery key secret. The recovery key is shown only once during setup and must be stored safely, for example with the Barangay Captain.
  e. Keep regular backups of the database file and store the backups securely.
  f. Report any suspected data breach, loss or unauthorized access to the barangay's designated Data Protection Officer without delay.
  g. Keep records only as long as necessary, and dispose of them securely when they are no longer needed.
